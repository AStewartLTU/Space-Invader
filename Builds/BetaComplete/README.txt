SPACE INVADERS BETA

Windows build: Space Invader Beta.exe

CONTROLS
A / Left Arrow: move left
D / Right Arrow: move right
Space: fire
Left Shift: activate the temporary shield
R: restart after a win or loss

OBJECTIVE
Destroy all 55 enemies to win. Avoid enemy bullets. If the formation reaches the player's row, the game ends.

BETA TEST NOTES
Check movement and screen edges, shooting and score, enemy fire and health, the shield's visible duration and cooldown, win/loss messages, and restart behavior. Please report any bugs you find.
